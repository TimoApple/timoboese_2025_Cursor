Timo Böse engineers visual narratives — grounded in graphic design, elevated by cinematic motion and technical expertise.

Focus

Every project gets the same obsessive attention. The output: 3D and 2D motion work built around purpose — not filler. From AI-generated video concepts to traditional motion graphics, the work spans commercial campaigns, TV idents, event openers, and experimental R&D.

Approach

Composition, typography, intent. The fundamentals drive the work — not the software. Combining classic graphic design principles with cutting-edge AI video tools and 3D animation to create distinctive visual narratives for brands.

Background

Trained in traditional graphic design. Years of professional practice spanning motion design, AI video production, creative direction, and visual design. Clients include Spotify, Google, Porsche, IBM, Adidas, LEGO, and ProSieben. Based in Berlin.
